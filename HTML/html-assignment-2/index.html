Introduction to Animals

Animals are multicellular organisms that belong to the kingdom Animalia. They are characterized by their ability to move, consume organic matter, and reproduce sexually. Animals can be found in a wide variety of habitats, from the depths of the oceans to the highest mountains.

Animals are classified into various groups based on their characteristics and evolutionary relationships.

Fun Facts

Thermophilic Bacteria are microorganisms that thrive in extremely hot environments, such as thermal vents on the ocean floor.The Temperature Range for These Bacteria is Between 50oC and 106oC.

Water(H2O) is essential for all known forms of life.
